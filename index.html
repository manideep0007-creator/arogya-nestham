Knowledge Protects

Myths vs Facts

Rural communities often hear traditional beliefs. Learn what is safe and when home remedies delay life-saving medical care.

Crucial Caution

Why You Must Not Self-Medicate

Taking medicines on your own or on shopkeeper recommendations is dangerous.

When you feel sick, buying tablets from a local shop without a doctor's prescription might provide temporary relief from pain or fever. However, this is a dangerous trap.
